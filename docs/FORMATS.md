# Supported conversions

Generated by `node scripts/format-matrix.cjs` from `electron/domain/capabilities.cjs`; do not edit by hand.
A direction is offered only when its engine is installed and passed its probe (ToolRegistry).
"Tested" = covered by `test/electron/engines.test.cjs`, `documents.test.cjs` or `ebooks.test.cjs` on a host with that engine; Windows-bundled
engines are tested by the Windows payload CI job (docs/TESTING.md).
"Tested" means a generated fixture went through the real engine and its output was checked for the
structure and content that direction should keep. It is not proof of perfect fidelity, nor of
every file people have: real-world documents (embedded fonts, images, tracked changes, scanned
PDFs) are not covered yet. Known losses are listed at the end and shown in the app where they matter.

## Known limits

Observed in the engine tests; the row category above still applies.

- **ebook -> docx**: Calibre keeps Hebrew text and marks it as Hebrew, but does not mark the paragraph right-to-left (`<w:bidi/>`), so Word lays it out left-to-right (seen with Calibre 7.6 on Linux; the app shows this note when DOCX is chosen for an e-book: `rtl-paragraphs-lost`).
- **ebook -> docx**: Calibre adds a "Table of Contents" page at the start.
- **sheet -> csv**: Only the first sheet; rows are padded with empty columns to the sheet's used width.
- **doc -> docx**: Hebrew sentences are split into several runs at punctuation and digits (text and direction are kept).
- **pdf -> docx / epub**: The layout is rebuilt from the PDF; tested with simple text pages only.
